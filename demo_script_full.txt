========================================================================
5 MINUTE DEMO SCRIPT — Sales Pipeline Lead Scorer
Single lead + bulk upload
========================================================================


BEFORE YOU RECORD
------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. CREDITS. Badge top right must say 5. If it says less you've already
   used some today and the bulk part won't run. This demo uses 4.

2. FILES ready on your desktop:
      sample_leads_5.csv   (to show the limit stopping you)
      sample_leads_3.csv   (the one that runs)

3. Log in before recording. Don't film the login.

4. THE WAITING. This is the thing to plan around.
      One lead      = about 52 seconds
      Three in bulk = about 2 and a half minutes
   You can talk over the single one, the script covers it. You can't
   talk for 2 and a half minutes over the bulk one, so record it
   normally and speed that bit up about 4x when you edit.
   Real recording lands around 7 minutes. Edited, about 5.

5. Read it out loud once before recording. If a line feels weird in
   your mouth, change it. It's your voice, not mine.


========================================================================
0:00 - 0:25     INTRO
ON SCREEN: the app, Add a lead page
========================================================================
"Hi, I'm Vivek. Today I'll show you a tool I built for sales teams.

So when leads come in, someone's got to figure out which ones are
actually worth calling, and then write to them. That eats hours.

This does both. You give it a lead, it looks them up, scores them out
of 100, and writes the email."


========================================================================
0:25 - 0:55     SETTINGS
ON SCREEN: menu > Settings > open "Your company & ICP"
========================================================================
"First it needs to know what you sell and who you're selling to. This
box here is probably the most important thing in the app.

You describe your company, then who's a good fit, who's a weak fit, and
who's just not a fit.

And that last bit is the one people skip. If you only describe your
perfect customer, everyone looks perfect. You've got to tell it who to
say no to. I'll come back to that at the end."


========================================================================
0:55 - 1:20     ADD ONE LEAD
ON SCREEN: menu > Add new lead. Fill the form while you talk.
========================================================================
"Okay so let me add a lead. Name, company, email.

This box is the use case. That's what they're trying to solve. Not what
I'm selling, what they actually need. That's what tells it whether my
product fits their problem or not.

And then Save and process."

  >> CLICK NOW. Clock starts.


========================================================================
1:20 - 2:20     WHILE IT RUNS   (about 52 seconds)
ON SCREEN: the spinner
========================================================================
"So while that's going, here's what's happening underneath.

There are four agents and they run one after another.

First one looks up the person. Their job, how senior they are, whether
they can actually sign off on anything.

Second one looks up the company. How big it is, what they do, whether
they match the profile I just wrote.

Third one takes both of those and scores it out of 100. And it's not
just a number, it breaks it down and tells you why.

The fourth only runs if the score's high enough. That one writes the
email.

And they're searching the web while they do this, so it's not working
off old training data. It's looking at what's there now."

  >> If it's still going, add:
     "The company research gets saved too, so the next lead from the
      same company skips a step and comes back quicker."


========================================================================
2:20 - 2:55     THE RESULT
ON SCREEN: the finished lead, expanded
========================================================================
"Right, done. There's the score.

And you can see why it got it. How senior the person is, company size,
how well they fit. Plus notes on anything that didn't add up.

And here's the email it wrote. It knows their job, their company, what
they asked about. I'd read that once and send it. Or edit it here if I
want to change something."


========================================================================
2:55 - 3:20     BULK: the limit stops me
ON SCREEN: Bulk import tab. Choose sample_leads_5.csv
========================================================================
"Now nobody really adds leads one at a time. You come back from an
event with a list.

So let me upload a file. This one's got five leads in it.

And it stops me. I've got four credits left, file's got five, so it
tells me straight away and asks for a smaller one.

Every lead costs real money to run, so there's a daily limit. And I'd
rather it tell me now than import everything and die halfway through."


========================================================================
3:20 - 3:40     BULK: running it
ON SCREEN: choose sample_leads_3.csv, click Import and process
========================================================================
"So, smaller file. Three leads, four credits.

Now watch this list. It does them one at a time and you can see each
one go from waiting, to being scored, to done.

I did that deliberately. One bar sitting at zero for three minutes
doesn't tell you anything."

  >> CLICK NOW. Speed this section up when you edit.


========================================================================
3:40 - 4:30     WHILE BULK RUNS   (2.5 min real, speed up to ~50s)
ON SCREEN: the per lead list ticking through
========================================================================
"Couple of things while these finish.

None of this actually runs in the web page. When I hit process it drops
the job in a queue and a separate worker picks it up. That's why the
app doesn't freeze, and I could close this tab right now and it'd still
finish.

And that limit from a second ago. Each lead's about three cents in AI
calls, and that's my money, not the user's. So without a cap, one
person uploading a thousand leads gets expensive fast. Everyone gets
five a day and it resets overnight.

I load tested it as well. Fifty people using it at once, no errors."


========================================================================
4:30 - 4:50     DASHBOARD
ON SCREEN: menu > Dashboard
========================================================================
"All three done.

And the dashboard gives you the overview. Scores, what industries
they're coming from, where they came from, how many over time.

So a manager can see what's going on without opening every single
lead."


========================================================================
4:50 - 5:00     THE ENDING
ON SCREEN: Lead details, high scores visible
========================================================================
"Last thing.

Look at these scores. Nearly everything's in the nineties. So I sat
down and scored twenty of them by hand myself, then compared.

The tool was coming out about fifteen points too high. And worse, it
was ranking them in nearly the wrong order.

I fixed most of that by writing down who's not a fit. It's still not
perfect. But I know exactly how imperfect, because I actually measured
it.

That's it. Thanks for watching."


========================================================================
IF YOU RUN LONG
========================================================================
Cut in this order:
  1. The dashboard bit (4:30).
  2. The load testing line.
  3. "Or edit it here if I want to change something."

Don't cut:
  - The use case explanation. It's why the scoring works at all.
  - The five lead file getting blocked. Free to show, nothing runs.
  - The ending.
